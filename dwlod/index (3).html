About Us

A United Voice for IT Dealers

AKITDA Ernakulam District Committee is the regional body of the All Kerala IT Dealers Association. Our mission is to foster a healthy, ethical, and progressive business environment for all members in the IT trade.

Community & Networking

Connect with fellow IT dealers, share insights, and collaborate on challenges and opportunities within the industry.

Advocacy & Support

We represent the interests of our members to government bodies, manufacturers, and distributors, addressing common issues.

Growth & Learning

Gain access to exclusive workshops, product training sessions, and market insights to help your business thrive.
